About the Project

WayToNoor — طريق النور is a global da'wah project under preparation: it presents Islam to those seeking it, from its trusted sources, and accompanies the new Muslim humanly — not mechanically — through the first thirty days and beyond, until he stands firm and continues his learning.

Method and sources

The content is grounded in trusted published sources (the Holy Qur'an, Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim with referencing via Dorar al-Sunniyya, and references such as IslamWeb and IslamQA), and the source is shown on screen and in line with every claim. We do not say about Allah what we do not know, and we never generate religious texts automatically. This project does not issue fatwas: rulings questions are referred to the established references.

Sharia review

Every piece passes Sharia and language review and the project owner's approval before any publication.

The journey and the handover to Thiqa

At the fourth rung (the Qur'an and Arabic from zero), the project hands the learner — with his consent and with his progress file — to Thiqa Academy, the specialised educational arm for teaching the Qur'an and Arabic to non-native speakers with live teachers; for this site is a da'wah and companionship project, not an academy.
