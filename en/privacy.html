Privacy Policy

This policy describes what data the WayToNoor website (waytonoor.com) collects from its visitors and what it does not collect, and how that data is used. Last updated: 27 November 2026.

What we never collect

This is a static website for reading and learning: it has no user accounts, no sign-in and no passwords; it sells nothing and receives no payments; and it does not accept donations — there is no channel for receiving funds in this project. We therefore never receive any financial data or account credentials from you.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to measure visits to this site anonymously — visit counts, pages read, and the sections most used — so we can understand what benefits visitors and improve the site for them. The service is configured as follows:

  • Every advertising purpose is denied by default: no advertising storage, no sharing of user data for advertising purposes, and no ad personalisation; we never use these statistics for advertising at all.
  • IP addresses are anonymised at measurement time.
  • The events measured are fixed categories from the site's own structure (a page read, a unit started, a quiz completed within a general score band); they never include personal data, never your quiz answers, and never anything you type into the site.
  • Reports may include aggregate demographic estimates (such as approximate age bands) provided by Google in general statistical form; they never identify any individual visitor and never reach us at the level of individuals.

Google Analytics uses statistical cookies to distinguish visits from one another; this site uses no advertising cookies. Analytics data is retained by Google for fourteen months under the analytics account settings and is then deleted automatically. We never sell this data and never publish it.

The “Ask us” form

The only channel through which data from you can reach us is the question form on the “Ask a Human” page: your name is optional — you may ask without naming yourself — your email address is required so we can reply, the topic is optional, and the text of your question. The form is delivered through the FormSubmit service to the project inbox waytonoor.official@gmail.com, and what you send is used only to reply to your question. Your question is not stored in any database on this website — the site has no server and no database — and what you send is never handed to any third party for any marketing purpose.

Some pages may show design-only preview forms for future services, labelled as design only; those forms neither send nor store any data.

What stays in your browser

Your browser stores, on your own device only — none of it ever reaches us — small operational data that keeps your reading and learning continuous:

  • Your progress through the units and the journey, and your best quiz scores (wtn.progress.v1).
  • Your reading position and last reached place in the Mushaf (wtn.reading.v1).
  • The state of the matching drills (wtn.match.v1).
  • Your practice-task confirmations and your optional notes on them (wtn.unitpractice.v1).
  • The reciter you chose for the Mushaf recitation (wtn_reciter).

This data stays on your device until you clear your browser data or this site's data in your browser settings, at which point it is all deleted immediately.

External services during use

  • Videos shown are embedded from YouTube in its privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie); we host no video on our site. If you play a video, your browser connects to YouTube and its privacy policy governs that playback.
  • The Mushaf audio recitation is streamed from the Islamic Network CDN (cdn.islamic.network); we do not host its audio files on our site. When you play the recitation, your browser connects to that network.
  • The site's text is displayed in your device's system fonts; we load no fonts from any external server.

Children's privacy

The site is educational and addressed to the general public seeking to learn about Islam; we do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and the only personal-data channel is the Ask form email described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a message reached us from a child and you want it deleted, write to the contact address below and it will be deleted.

Contact about privacy

For any question about this policy, or to request deletion of a message you sent us, write to: waytonoor.official@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and change the “last updated” date at its top; no change applies to data collected before its publication except with its owner's consent.
