Your First 30 Days

The “First 30 Days” journey on the ladder that leads to the Light: five rungs, and one curriculum at two speeds — self-paced learning, always open, at your rhythm, and a live cohort with a named companion at launch. Each rung is completed by the standard written in its own texts: its lesson quizzes averaging at least 80% (attempts in sequence, a new attempt opening only after a guided review task, the best attempt counting) plus completion of all its performed practice tasks. Then the rung lights up and the next one opens — and no certificate of any kind is issued; the lit rung is the mark. In this preview your progress is saved in your browser only.
