Your First 30 Days

The “First 30 Days” journey on the ladder that leads to the Light: five rungs, and one curriculum at two speeds — self-paced learning, always open, at your rhythm, and a live cohort with a named companion at launch. Each rung is completed by the standard written in its own texts: its lesson quizzes averaging at least 80% (attempts in sequence, a new attempt opening only after a guided review task, the best attempt counting) plus completion of all its performed practice tasks. Then the rung lights up and the next one opens — and no certificate of any kind is issued; the lit rung is the mark. In this preview your progress is saved in your browser only.

Your next step

You have reached the Light — your next step is with a teacher

Arabic is the language of your new religion — in it you read your Lord's Book and understand your prayer. And the Qur'an is the Muslim's Book — learned to be his guide through all of life.

At Thiqa Academy you pursue both with a live teacher in one-to-one live classes: recitation and pronunciation correction from zero, graduated memorisation from the short surahs and beyond, or systematic Arabic from the letters to reading and conversation. Your Companion here does not leave you at the door: he accompanies your path decision, and your progress file moves only with your explicit consent, so your teacher starts from where you reached — not from zero.
