The WayToNoor Community — the Graduate's Home

A design page (front-end only): the community is an internal space that opens at launch for the people of the journey — this preview shows its shape and frame; no registration or posting works here.

When you graduate from the fifth rung you do not walk out into a void: inside this project you have a digital home whose door does not close — a private space for new Muslims like you, where you write about your new life and its challenges honestly among people living exactly what you live, with rooms and meetings with sheikhs and teachers who answer and guide, and your own companion present there among your brothers and sisters. You enter it today as a receiver, and tomorrow you inhabit it as a support for those climbing behind you.

Write about your new life

Members' posts about their steps, challenges and daily questions — among people who understand because they are living the same thing.

Rooms and meetings

Scheduled rooms and meetings with sheikhs, teachers and companions: answers, guidance and company — no fatwa inside the community; rulings questions are referred to the established references.

Your siblings on the ladder

Those who climbed the rungs before you and after you: practical and moral support, and a conscious support network instead of post-decision loneliness.

The safeguarding frame

  • Declared human moderation of content and conversations, with immediate escalation to the supervisor at any violation.
  • Gender-appropriate spaces under the project's rules, and same-gender companionship.
  • No fatwa inside the community, ever — rulings questions are referred to IslamQA and IslamWeb.
  • The new Muslim's privacy is sacred: no member's story is used outside the community except with his explicit, revocable consent.
  • Minors are outside the companionship programme and the community until a child-safeguarding policy is adopted.
