The nature of the site and its content

WayToNoor is an educational da'wah project: it presents Islam to those seeking it and accompanies the new Muslim, from trusted sources that are shown on the pages and in line with every claim. The project does not issue individual fatwas; questions of Sharia rulings are referred to the established references such as IslamQA and IslamWeb. The content is offered for discovery and learning, and does not replace asking people of knowledge about rulings that concern you personally.
